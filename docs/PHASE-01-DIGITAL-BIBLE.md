# 3RD WORLD — Digital Bible v1

## Core principle
Big visuals. Small interface. Fast commerce.

The customer should experience the product and the brand before the interface.

## Visual system
- Core: WORLD BLACK / OFF WHITE
- Drop accents are temporary; core UI stays timeless.
- Neutral grotesk UI typography.
- Product imagery and campaign imagery remain visually distinct.
- Motion is restrained and functional.
- Mobile is a primary storefront, not a scaled desktop.

## Experience separation
- SHOP = transaction
- WORLD = culture
- ARCHIVE = history
- ACCESS = anticipation
- PASSPORT = customer relationship

## Non-negotiables
- No fake scarcity.
- No fake viewers.
- No visual clutter around checkout.
- No template-first design.
- No required intro animation.
- No raw payment-card storage.
